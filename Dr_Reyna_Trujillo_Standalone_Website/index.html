Teaching, learning and instructional leadership

From early childhood and K–12 classrooms to community colleges: Adjunct Professor, Los Angeles Community College District (2016–present) at East LA College, West LA College and LA Valley College. She teaches credit, noncredit, workforce, continuing education and dual enrollment courses in face-to-face, online, hybrid and HyFlex formats.

Education is a transformative force. Her philosophy blends student-centered learning, structured guidance, experiential application, reflective practice and growing learner independence, grounded in child development, adult learning theory, culturally responsive pedagogy and workforce education.

  • Competency-based experiences connecting theory with professional practice
  • Generative AI woven into teaching, assessment and faculty development
  • Culturally responsive practice for multilingual, adult, first-generation and career-changing learners
  • Instruction aligned to workforce competencies and labor market needs
